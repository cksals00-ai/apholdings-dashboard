02 · Philosophy

Three things.

We don't put AI in a person's place. We don't take from users. What we don't know, we say we don't know.

1

We don't put AI in a person's place

Not work people couldn't do — work they had stopped doing. 250,000 products, 2,455 stocks — only where a human limit stopped the work. Judgment stays with people.

2

We don't take from users

No accounts · no ads · no data collection. Records stay on the device and disappear when the app is deleted.

3

What we don't know, we say we don't know

“There was no formula for picking surging stocks in advance” — the conclusion of two years of full-market analysis, published as is. An AI that states its limits instead of pretending certainty.

No promises we can't keep

Zero accounts · zero ads · zero data collection. As the company grows, these three stay.
