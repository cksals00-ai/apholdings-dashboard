Founder’s story

It started with the kids.

Most of what A.P Holdings builds began with the kids at home.

Shopping with a child who has atopic dermatitis means reading the back of every snack bag first. The print is small, and the ingredients to avoid hide under different names. Instead of reading every label ourselves, we built a tool that checks against Korea’s MFDS public data and answers “this contains it”, with the evidence. That became AP Safe (Safelist).

With a child who loves basketball, we made an exercise promise: run 30 minutes, 100 times, and there is a present. Wear the watch and go out; at 30 minutes it taps your wrist, and when you get home a stamp appears on the board. A roulette and cheering messages keep the middle from getting dull. It was an app for one household — until we realized every home has a promise like this. So we are preparing it as the Dad & Kid Project.

The four tiger siblings of Hangeul Cubs, and LAST WAVE, which shows a child building a game with AI, came from the same place.

We made this our company’s principle. Put AI where people used to give up, keep the evidence public, and leave the decision to people. When something that worked in one home works in another, it becomes a business.

02 · Philosophy

Three things.

We don't put AI in a person's place. We don't take from users. What we don't know, we say we don't know.

1

We don't put AI in a person's place

Not work people couldn't do — work they had stopped doing. 250,000 products, 2,455 stocks — only where a human limit stopped the work. Judgment stays with people.

2

We don't take from users

No accounts · no ads · no data collection. Records stay on the device and disappear when the app is deleted.

3

What we don't know, we say we don't know

“There was no formula for picking surging stocks in advance” — the conclusion of two years of full-market analysis, published as is. An AI that states its limits instead of pretending certainty.

0 · 0 · 0

No promises we can't keep

Zero accounts · zero ads · zero data collection. As the company grows, these three stay.
