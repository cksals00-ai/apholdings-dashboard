Four tiger siblings

Hangeul Cubs

Daho · Kkobi · Aari · Rami.

The tiger siblings who teach Hangeul in the Hangeul Cubs app and on YouTube. One bottom block changes a word’s meaning — the four of them split the lessons between them.

Daho

Eldest · host and wrap-up

The big brother who starts every lesson. “One block at the bottom — that’s all it takes.”

Kkobi

Second · words and sounds

Splits ㄴ and ㄹ with the tip of the tongue. Loves words, snacks and a taekwondo kick.

Aari

Third · pronunciation and songs

Makes the ㅇ block ring like a bell. Pronunciation coach and singer.

Rami

Youngest · writing and cheering

In charge of the sounds that stop short. Writing and cheering.
