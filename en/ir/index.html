01 · Investment thesis

Not for lack of information. Things stop for lack of a decision.

Choosing a snack or setting a room rate — it is the same. AP puts data, expert criteria, procedure and software at that point and turns it into a repeatable decision system. A decision structure built once is reused in the next product.
