01 / INVESTMENT THESIS

We turn complexity into systems.

Reduce the recurring complexity between information and action across industries. Turn learning from use into reusable products, processes and IP. Three portfolios apply the same principle to different problems.

Across industries, complexity often remains between information and action. AP Holdings was created to turn that complexity into repeatable systems.

COMPANY PRINCIPLE / GLOBAL-BY-DESIGN

Built in Korea.
Designed for the world.

Every AP product is Global-by-Design.

Our products are designed from the start for multiple markets, languages, currencies and policies. Separating core product logic from market configuration and local integrations is a company-wide principle.

GLOBAL CORE

Reusable product logic

MARKET CONFIGURATION

Locale, currency, timezone and policy

LOCAL ADAPTERS

Payment, logistics, channels and experts

A reusable core. A versioned Market Pack.

A versioned bundle of configuration, content and partners connects the core to a new market. Local review and pilots come before expansion.

Market research → expert and policy review → testing → pilot → activation → review

This is our design principle and expansion direction, not a claim that every product already operates in multiple countries.
