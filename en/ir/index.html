01 · Investment thesis

Not for lack of information. Things stop for lack of a decision.

Choosing a snack or buying a Korean product from abroad — it is the same. AP puts data, expert criteria, procedure and software at that point and turns it into a repeatable decision system. A decision structure built once is reused in the next product.

  • Ask
  • Check against existing records
  • Answer with evidence
  • A person decides
  • Record the outcome

03 · Five businesses and revenue

Five businesses, three revenue streams.

Intelligence: partner subscriptions and connectors. Shop: direct sales and commissions. Intelligence also supports franchising. Edu · Games: in-app purchases and educational quiz packs. Entertainment · News is the channel that brings people to all of them. No advertising and no sale of user data.

Intelligence

Partner subscriptions · connectors

Subscriptions that connect Safelist and LightList data to partner services. The connectors are free to build a user base, leading on to the safe market and Hospital Report Pro.

Shop

Direct sales · commissions · franchising

Direct sales through Lia Select and Shopee, partner commissions from Agoda · Klook · Coupang, and AP SELECT franchise fees, royalties and system fees.

Edu · Games

IAP · education B2B

Hangeul Cubs lesson packs, QuizRanker quiz packs for schools, academies and companies, LAST WAVE skins and passes (no pay-to-win).

Common

Data

Who asked what, chose what and bought what, linked under one ID. The basis for the next product and curation.
