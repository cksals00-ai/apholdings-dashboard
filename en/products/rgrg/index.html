QuizRanker

A live online quiz battle platform anyone, any age, can pick up and enjoy — where general knowledge grows naturally as you play.

Not an educational game — a game for everyone. Learning is one part of it.

01 · Why we made it

A quiz is play, not study.

A game anyone can enjoy comes first. General knowledge is what grows, naturally, while you play. Education is one part of it.

02 · For all ages

Kids and parents, friends and coworkers — one match.

A room that asks neither your age nor your level. Family, friends and colleagues on the same stage.

03 · Knowledge, naturally

Every lost round arms the next one.

Nothing to memorise — just the urge to win. Missed questions go to the mistake notebook and come back in the next match. Current affairs, history, science, culture, riddles.

04 · A live online platform

Not a workbook you do alone — a stage you share.

Multiple choice · O/X · Initials · Speed · Trivia · Random — a different kind of fun in every room. A platform where others compete on the questions you write.

05 · Characters

A character that plays as you, titles that build up.

Eight characters and titles. Nothing you can buy wins a match.

06 · Now

iPhone · preparing for release.

A live online quiz battle anyone, any age, can enjoy.
