RGRG

The scholarship quiz show, in your hand.

A TV quiz-show studio in your phone. From riddles to Korean history, languages and certifications — learn by competing.

01 · The stage

Question up — hit the buzzer.

Six players on one stage. Only the first to buzz may answer. Five seconds. Wrong costs points; right earns the time left on the clock. Fast beats knowledgeable.

02 · What kind of questions

From riddles to Korean history.

Nine topics. Easy · normal · hard. Ten questions per round, three minutes.

03 · In the classroom

A lesson becomes a match.

The teacher sets the questions and the whole class takes the stage. Preview and review habits show up as scores, and missed questions go straight to the mistake notebook.

Classroom

04 · One round

One round, five minutes.

Main game · today's mini-game · a bet on the last question. There is a place where third becomes first.

05 · Writing questions

Others compete on the questions I wrote.

Write a question, earn credits. Multiple choice or true/false. That is why the questions never run dry.

06 · Quiz packs

Same game, different subject.

One game. What you swap is the quiz pack. No rebuilding the game for each country.
