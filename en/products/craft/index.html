01 · The problem

The skill exists. No one to carry it on.

Limited outlets and large distribution margins. Creating alone cannot sustain a living. Fewer of each generation choose a craft that demands years of training. The works exist, but there is no channel or trust mechanism to reach overseas buyers.

収 · Income

Unstable income

Limited outlets, large distribution margins. Creating alone cannot sustain a living.

傳 · Succession

No successors

A generation that does not choose crafts requiring years of training. The skill breaks off.

市 · Market

No route to market

The works exist, but no channel or trust mechanism reaches overseas buyers.

眞 · Authenticity

The authenticity problem

Cheap imports and masters' originals share the same Insadong shelf. No way to tell them apart.

02 · The opportunity

A window opened by Hallyu.

K-pop, dramas and films lead to demand for traditional craft. 12.8 million visitors in Jan–Jul 2026, +21.3% year on year. Demand for the “real thing”, not mass-produced souvenirs, is steady.

流 · Spreading interest

  1. K-pop · dramas · films → demand for traditional craft
  2. 12.8 million visitors (Jan–Jul 2026, +21.3%) — Korea Tourism Organization

眞 · The “real thing” premium

  1. Masters' originals, unlike mass-produced souvenirs
  2. Steady demand for works with signature and making record

船 · Mature infrastructure

  1. International shipping · online payment · multilingual commerce
  2. Even small sellers can reach overseas markets

廣 · The channel already exists

  1. LIA's channel — English and Vietnamese viewers
  2. An introduction path through apps, website and blog
03 · How

尋 · 錄 · 廣 · 賣 Find, record, promote, sell.

Phase 1 is trust before sales. First the world learns “this master exists.” Sales open once the response is confirmed.

尋 · Phase 1

Find the artisans

Intangible cultural heritage holders, masters and traditional craftspeople. Credentials such as national or provincial designation stated.

錄 · Phase 1

Record the making

The process and the story on video and photo. Every work signed, with a making record.

廣 · Phase 1

Promote overseas

To the world through LIA's multilingual channel. English → Chinese → Japanese.

賣 · Phase 2

Connect the sale

Commission-based sales after response is confirmed. Workshop visits during a trip → online purchase after returning home.

04 · Items and difference

A structure that proves authenticity.

Celadon and white porcelain, mother-of-pearl lacquerware and woodwork, hanji lamps and fans. Only artisan-made pieces distinct from cheap imports. Every work with signature, making record and provenance.

陶 · 工 · 紙 — Items

  1. Ceramics — celadon · white porcelain
  2. Craftwork — mother-of-pearl lacquerware · woodwork
  3. Hanji — lamps · fans

證 · 影 · 署 — Proof

  1. Artisan credentials — national or provincial designation stated
  2. Process record — the making captured on video
  3. Provenance — signature and making record on every work. The basis for a premium price

Existing routes

  1. Government bodies — competitions and exhibitions, not year-round, B2B buyers
  2. Insadong — physical shops, mixed origins, gift demand
  3. Overseas marketplaces — individual seller listings, no verification

K-Craft

  1. Year-round D2C storytelling with proof of authenticity
  2. Individual Hallyu fans as buyers
  3. Selling the artisan's life and skill together with the work
05 · All three gain

Artisan, platform, buyer.

Artisans gain fair income and the means to train successors, the platform gains transaction fees and an artisan network, overseas buyers gain verified originals and a trustworthy origin.

AP does not provide appraisal, insurance or customs itself. It records, connects and coordinates, with specialist partners.
