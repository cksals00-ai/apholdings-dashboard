AP Safe

Know what to avoid.

The avoid list saved on my phone filters what the AI shows, and only unflagged items get picked. From ingredient scanner to AI-assisted safe commerce.

Safelist app

The existing Safelist app is evidence of building. Its currently available features are distinct from the broader AP Safe product concept.

Existing connector access
01 · One scene

Siri, a week of snacks safe for my child.

Before anything is bought, any AI asks Safelist first. Only unflagged items reach the cart.

02 · Why Safelist

AI is probability. Ingredients are fact.

AI can sound confident about what it doesn't know. For a child's food, that is the real danger. Safelist checks the source records and hands the AI facts only.

Not a diagnosis · Public database · Final call stays with the parent

03 · Where the data lives

My body's data stays on my phone.

Child profiles, photos and logs never leave the device. The server receives only the label and the avoid list, then discards them.

04 · Clothing, too

100% cotton? The label tells you.

Snap the care label or share a store link, and Safelist reads which fibers, at what percent. Fibers you avoid (default: polyester, nylon, acrylic, wool) show 🔴. For choosing clothes for a child with atopic skin.

Based on the seller's label · actual fabric cannot be verified · final call by the guardian · Safelist 1.5

  • Snap label · share link · paste text
  • Read on this phone (nothing sent)
  • Fiber % · avoided fibers 🔴
  • Show 100%-cotton labels only
05 · Web pilot

Before you recommend, check the conflicts first.

Compares product composition against a user's avoid and caution conditions, and applies the same rules to alternative candidates. A workspace tool for organizations — separate from the app and connector.

A review tool that compares entered conditions with composition data. Not a medical diagnosis or an absolute safety guarantee.

Separates conflict conditions that exclude a recommendation from information that needs a further check. Reviewers see the reasoning and record accept, edit or reject, plus observed outcomes.

  • Excel input · reasoning · reviewer records
