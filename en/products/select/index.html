AP SELECT

A store designed for choosing.

Not a store that sells things, but a store that chooses. Advice and trial in store. Purchase online.

01

It asks first. What do you avoid?

Milk or wheat to avoid, a budget, a preference for fragrance-free — the consultant knows first. Then only what fits stays on the shelf. When something new passes your criteria, you hear first.

02

The store is for advice and trial. There is no checkout.

See, touch and try three or four in person. If you like one, a single QR — order online, delivered home.

03 · FRANCHISE

We provide a system, not inventory.

Headquarters provides the choosing system; franchisees provide space and consultation. Franchising opens only after company-run stores prove it.

Concept & System Design stage. No franchise recruitment or expert contracts yet; disclosed only after validation.
