Hangeul Cubs

Learn Hangeul with four tiger siblings.

For foreigners and children meeting Korean for the first time. One batchim per episode. English · Vietnamese · French.

01 · Four siblings

One final consonant each, four siblings share the teaching.

Daho covers the basics of batchim, Kkobi ㄴ and ㄹ, Aari the ㅇ ending, Rami the ㄱ · ㄷ · ㅂ stops. One batchim per episode.

DAHO · eldest

Daho

The basics of batchim. One block underneath — that's all.

KKOBI · second

Kkobi

ㄴ and ㄹ. Hear the sounds split at the tip of the tongue.

AARI · third

Aari

The ㅇ ending. Ringing like a bell: ng, ng, ng.

RAMI · youngest

Rami

ㄱ · ㄷ · ㅂ stops. Short, and cut off clean.

02 · Learn · listen · solve

Three screens, one flow.

The signature: final consonants shown in a separate color. 반/방 · 발/밥 · 문/물 · 곰/공 — sounds where one batchim changes the meaning. Ending with a first sentence you will actually use: “물 주세요.”

42 lessonsEN · VI · FR hintsOn-device pronunciationFully offlineNo ads · no accounts
03 · Challenge

Compete on what you learned.

Lessons are for learning; the challenge is for competing. Three rounds, twelve questions, about two minutes — warm-up · today's mini-game · final bet. Unlocks after finishing at least one lesson.

  • R1 Warm-up · 5 questions · 10 seconds
  • R2 Today's mini-game · true/false · flash · elimination
  • R3 Final bet · stake your points to double them
Faster means more pointsFlames from two in a rowQuestions only from finished lessons
