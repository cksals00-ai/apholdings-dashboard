AI news pours out every week. Models launch, prices change, new features appear. Yet after reading it all, the question left over is always the same: **so what changes in my work?**

AP News is a board built to answer that question. A.P Holdings runs apps, commerce and AI character channels with a small team and AI. We read the AI news each week not to show off what we know, but to decide how we will work next week. We publish that decision-making process as it happens.

## How we write

- **Weekly AI news**: We narrow the week's big announcements to three or fewer, and for each one we separate "Facts / Interpretation / What we would do."
- **Insight**: We take the question one piece of news raises about how we work and explore it at length.
- **Practice**: We document the tools and methods we actually use, so you can follow along.

## Our principles

1. **We cite our sources.** We only write figures and dates that you can verify in the sources at the end of each piece. When something cannot be verified, we say "we could not verify it."
2. **We do not mix fact and opinion.** What was announced and our interpretation go in separate paragraphs.
3. **We write with AI, and a human reviews.** Drafts are made together with AI, and an editor checks the facts before publishing. Every article is labeled that way.
4. **This is not investment advice.** We do not write pieces that promise or recommend stock prices, tickers or returns.
5. **We write only when it connects to our work.** We do not cover something just because it is trending.

## Who should read this

People who build and run products alone or in small teams and wonder every week how far they can hand work over to AI. We write it so that you do not need to be a developer to follow along.
