K-Ranker Privacy Policy

Provider: AP Holdings. Updated 7 October 2026.

K-Ranker offers English quizzes about Korean language, K-pop, food and culture, with practice and guest real-time duels.

Practice information on your device

The app stores your welcome preference, selected character and practice results on your device. Practice records are separate from online duel data. You can remove practice results using My progress → Clear practice history.

Online duel information

Entering live play creates a guest identifier, automatically assigned display name and a secret guest token. The token is stored in your device’s Keychain. The server stores a hash of the token to authorize your requests. Room membership, chosen topic and mode, quiz submissions, scores and game times are processed to provide, secure and score duels. Other players see your display name and score, but do not receive your secret token.

Hosting and abuse prevention

Online duels run on Supabase infrastructure used by AP Holdings. K-Ranker uses separate game tables from the domestic QuizRanker game; it does not provide a shared account or wallet. To limit abuse, the service processes a keyed hash of the connection IP address, guest-creation timestamps, request counts and timing. The game database does not store the raw IP address. Hosting providers may process connection and security logs separately.

Expiration and removal

Guest credentials expire after 30 days and duel rooms become inaccessible after one hour. Expired guest and room records become eligible for cleanup after a further day. Cleanup currently runs when new guests are created; physical removal does not have a guaranteed daily schedule. Creation-limit records use a rolling 24-hour window; stale hashed connection buckets are eligible for cleanup after two days.

Use Privacy & data → Delete online guest data to request deletion of the guest identity associated with this device. The app removes its secret token after the server confirms deletion. Single-player room entries are deleted. In a shared room, your identity is replaced by an anonymous “Deleted guest” label and the room ends; the other player’s score remains. Clearing local practice history does not delete online duel data, and deleting online guest data does not clear local practice history.

Accounts, advertising and purchases

This version does not require an account or request an email address for guest duels. It contains no advertising SDKs and no in-app purchases.

Your questions and requests

Contact AP Holdings for privacy questions or data requests. If you can still access your device, the in-app deletion control verifies ownership without exposing your secret token. Never send your secret token to another person.
