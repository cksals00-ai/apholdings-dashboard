HOW TO PLAY

Your first match, made clear.

01

Choose a room and mode

Choose a head-to-head or group match. Depending on matchmaking, you can practise against AI.

02

Press when you know

Claiming the buzzer opens the choices. Answer within the displayed time. Wrong answers and timeouts lose points.

03

Learn from each match

Review accuracy, win rate and match history. Revisit missed questions in your wrong-answer notes.

True-or-false and Speed let everyone answer without a buzzer. Each mode shows its time limit and scoring rules.
