K-Scan — Test Barcodes for App Review

A.P Holdings · for App Store Review use

Why this page exists. K-Scan reads barcodes on Korean packaged food. Reviewers outside Korea are unlikely to have such a product at hand, so the three barcodes below are real EAN-13 codes taken from the app's bundled database.

How to test:

  1. Open this page on any second screen (another phone, tablet, or monitor). Printing is not required.
  2. Launch K-Scan and point the camera at a barcode below.
  3. The product card appears with name, manufacturer, type, and ingredients.

This works with the device in Airplane Mode. The 258,000-product database is bundled inside the app; product lookup never touches the network.
