없습니다. 이 앱은 어떤 개인정보도 수집·저장·전송·판매·공유하지 않습니다. 회원가입이 없으며 이름, 이메일, 전화번호, 기기 식별자를 요구하지 않습니다.

확인 기록(붙여넣은 원재료 문구, 판정 결과, 제품 이름, 사용 후 남긴 피드백)도 이 기기에만 저장됩니다.

이 정보들은 인터넷으로 전송되지 않습니다. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 삭제되며, 개발자를 포함해 누구도 열람할 수 없습니다.

세이프리스트는 인터넷 통신을 하지 않습니다. 모든 판정은 기기 안에서 이루어집니다. 서버가 존재하지 않으므로 데이터가 외부로 나갈 경로 자체가 없습니다.

없습니다. 외부 SDK, 추적 도구, 분석 도구, 광고 네트워크를 일절 사용하지 않습니다.

아동

Safelist — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Safelist collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no servers.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, or device identifiers.

What the app stores, and where

Your avoid list (the ingredients you choose to avoid, including any you type in) and your check history (pasted ingredient text, results, product names, and any feedback you leave) are stored only in this app's private area on your device. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them. No one, including the developer, can access them.

Network use

Safelist does not use the internet. All matching happens on your device. There is no server, so there is no path for your data to leave the phone.

Third-party services

None. No SDKs, no trackers, no analytics, no ads.

Children

The app is built for caregivers checking food ingredients for a child. It knowingly collects no data from anyone, including children — because it collects no data at all.

Medical notice

Safelist is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prescribe. It compares the ingredient text printed on a product against a list you define yourself. It cannot detect cross-contamination during manufacturing or errors in labeling. Always consult your child's doctor for decisions about diet and treatment.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
